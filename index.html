Niche Research

Market research for digital products

Niche Research is a market research tool that analyzes publicly available marketplace and web data to identify trends, pricing patterns, customer needs, and opportunities for digital products.

About

Niche Research aggregates publicly available data from various online sources and turns it into structured market research. The tool helps creators of digital products understand demand, evaluate niches, and make informed decisions based on open market signals rather than guesswork.

What we analyze

Marketplace trends
Product categories
Pricing patterns
Search interest
Publicly available customer discussions
Competitive landscape

Data usage

Niche Research works exclusively with publicly available data. The tool is not designed to access, modify, or store private data of users or sellers. It does not collect personal accounts data, private messages, or any information that is not openly published on the web.
